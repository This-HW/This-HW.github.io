Agent operations platform

AI plans, executes and evaluates; people act as a board, deciding only on direction, budget and risk (in progress)

Context

As I delegated more work to AI, more of my time went into checking the results, and human review became the bottleneck.

Problem

For AI to plan work, carry it out and judge the results without a person in the loop, I had to decide what a person must still keep control of.

What I builtsecond version in progress

  • People act as a board and decide only on direction, budget and risk.
  • For each piece of work, the hypothesis and the success and failure criteria are fixed before any result exists.
  • AI runners work in isolated workspaces; the author and the reviewer are always different runners.
  • Actions that affect external systems pass through a single gateway, and a person approves by phone where required.
  • Version one assigned local and cloud models by role, falling back to the next model on failure.

What I got wrong, then fixed

  • Version one split the work across seven AI workers on a queue. It crash-looped more than 200,000 times, so I replaced it with a single build flow.
  • A local-model switch I had asked for myself failed the rejection criteria I set before measuring (3.26× slower, nearly twice the memory), so I withdrew it.
  • Since then, before changing anything, I first write down what result would make me abandon the change.

Result

4,520 commits (as of 2026-11-08) and 15,409 test functions (as of 2026-10-28), across both versions. The first pilot will be the data-to-news pipeline (not yet connected as of Nov 2026).
